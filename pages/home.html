Arko Datta

PhD Scholar at SeNSE Lab, IIT Madras


Research Areas: Wireless Sensing, RF Localization, Digital Twins

I am a first year PhD Scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Madras, working in the Sensing and Networked Systems Engineering (SeNSE) Lab under the guidance of Dr. Ayon Chakraborty. My work focuses on enhancing the quality of experience in wireless networking and sensing applications by leveraging visual data and digital twins.

Before my PhD, I completed my Master's in Technology from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Durgapur, mentored by Dr. Sujoy Saha and co-mentored by Prof. Subrata Nandi. There, I developed a strong foundation in Systems Design through my work with the Systems Research Lab.

My academic journey began with a Bachelor's in Technology from the School of Computer Science and Engineering at KIIT-DU. I completed my schooling from M.P. Birla Foundation Higher Secondary School.

Although I hail from Kolkata, India: the city of joy, I have spent most of my adult life away from home, but always staying true to my roots. Besides work, I really enjoy music and love playing the keyboards and the harmonica.
