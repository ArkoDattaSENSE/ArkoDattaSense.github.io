Arko Datta

PhD Scholar at SeNSE Lab, IIT Madras

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Madras working in Sensing and Networked Systems Engineering (SeNSE) Lab, guided by Dr. Ayon Chakraborty. I am building systems that enhance the quality of experience in wireless networking and sensing applications by leveraging visual data and digital twins.
I completed my Masters in Technology from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, National Institute of Technology, Durgapur under the guidance of Dr. Sujoy Saha and co-mentored by Prof. Subrata Nandi, where I worked with the Systems Research Lab and earned my foundations in Systems Design.
I have done my Bachelors in Technology from the School of Computer Science and Engineering, KIIT-DU. I have completed my entire school life in M.P. Birla Foundation Higher Secondary School.
Though I hail from the city of joy, Kolkata, India, I have spent most of my adult life away from the city, learning from different cultures, but still staying true to my roots.
